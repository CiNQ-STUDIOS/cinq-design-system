Easings

The six library curves. Anything else fails motion:check. Press play to run every ball with its curve over --dur-cinematic.

out
cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)
canonical. expo.out. Everything that moves on its own.
in-out
cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)
canonical. Things that open then close.
in
cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.84, 0)
derived. Exits, expo.in.
emphasized
cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)
derived. Sheets and route travel.
back
cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)
canonical. back.out(1.6), the mark's segments.
linear
cubic-bezier(0, 0, 1, 1)
canonical. Scrubbed tweens and the marquee only.
